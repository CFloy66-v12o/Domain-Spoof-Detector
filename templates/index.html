Suspicious Domain Checker

Examine a domain for Unicode look-alike characters, Punycode, mixed writing systems, and common typosquatting patterns.

Text-only analysis: This checker does not visit, resolve, download from, or otherwise connect to the submitted domain. Do not enter passwords, authentication codes, private document links, password-reset links, or URLs containing personal access tokens.

Only the hostname is retained for display and analysis.

Enter the domain you believe the submitted address may be imitating—for example, paypal.com. This enables additional look-alike and misspelling checks and may substantially change the result.
